Wizard Chess

Duel a rival wizard across the board, and bend the rules of chess with magic. Make knights move like kings, or let rooks levitate over pieces.

Begin the duel

Spells

Bend the rules on your own pieces. Knights can move like kings, rooks can levitate, and pawns can slip back in time.

Hexes

Curse the enemy wizard's pieces. Slow them down, stop them from capturing a type, or freeze them in place.

Mana

Every spell and hex costs mana and uses your turn. Everyone regains 1 mana every 2 moves, up to 6.
